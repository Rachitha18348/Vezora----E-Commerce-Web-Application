Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VEZORA, orders, payments, delivery, and returns.

How can I place an order?

Browse our products, open the product you want, add it to your cart, and proceed through checkout.

What payment methods are available?

VEZORA supports online payments through Razorpay. Razorpay provides available options such as UPI, cards, and net banking. Cash on Delivery is also available.

Can I pay using UPI?

Yes. Select Online Payment during checkout and continue to Razorpay. You can then select UPI if it is available in the Razorpay checkout.

Is Cash on Delivery available?

Yes. Select Cash on Delivery during checkout to place your order without making an online payment.

How can I track my order?

After placing an order, go to My Orders and select View Order Details. You can then view the current order status and tracking information.

Where can I view my previous orders?

After logging in, open My Orders from your account dashboard to view your order history.

What happens if my payment fails?

If an online payment is unsuccessful, your order is not completed as a paid order. You can return to the payment page and try again.

How can I contact VEZORA support?

Visit the Contact Us page and send us your question. Our support team will get back to you.
